# Corrections and cancellation

Apply the ordered migrations through `20261011001200_corrections.sql`. Ordinary clients retain public configuration only. No new credentials, services, administrator installation or hosted deployment are required.

Open **Corrections and cancellation** from a trip, or **Corrections and history** from Expenses. The latter includes standalone expenses and a paginated, office-scoped trip chooser. History keeps the original event, recorded time, actor, reason and reversal/replacement links. Voided/replaced expenses remain visible as historical records and their private attachments remain accessible under current office permissions.

For a correction, enter a reason and review the corrected amount/date and allocations. Fare corrections use the existing quote/brokerage method and server calculation. Replacing an expense preserves its historical owner when its vehicle/trip is unchanged. Explicitly review each dependent settlement before confirming; incorrect dependent expenses must first receive their own independent correction. An ambiguous response retains the same reviewed command for retry. A conflict requires closing, reloading and reviewing; no overwrite occurs. Success means the transaction committed.

Cancellation requires origin permission and the trip creator, or an administrator. Enter explicit agreed charges and broker shares for every remaining consignment, including zero charges. The server computes owner shares, closes original outstanding earning claims and creates identified customer credits and owner/broker recovery claims where appropriate. Actual collections, settlements, dispatch/arrival dates, expenses, cars, receipt files and source records remain recorded. The adjustment uses its explicit cancellation date; a correction reversal mirrors its source date and a replacement uses the corrected value's date. A fare correction retains the dispatch recognition date; changing operational dispatch dates continues through the lifecycle workflow.

Refunds require a separate review and available held funds. **Receive owner / broker recovery** records an actual return of overpaid entitlement. **Personally fund a customer refund** records newly supplied personal money as custody plus a separate funding payable; it does not consume customer credit or create a refund. Confirm the actual refund separately. Later actual recoveries can repay the funding payable through **Recover personal funding**. Financing is distinct from consuming a claim: retrying one financing command does not post it again, and each actual refund/recovery allocation consumes its own available claims once.

Cancellation fee entries can themselves be corrected with a reason and an explicit unwind of dependent refunds/recoveries. Corrections to other financial records on a cancelled trip are blocked when they would invalidate its reviewed cancellation adjustment. Independently correcting a self-funded cost with no financial claims remains possible. These blocks preserve money and require resolving the recorded dependencies rather than bypassing them. Previously issued snapshots and permanent document numbers remain unchanged; document issuance/superseding presentation is T13.

The backend exposes `review_financial_correction`, `correct_financial_event`, `review_cancellation`, `cancel_and_adjust` and `list_financial_history`. Inputs accept typed business fields, exact decimal strings and review/version information. They never accept arbitrary journals, actors or unrestricted business/office scope. Dependency and claim revisions are checked under the same business/account lock used by posting and account administration. Reversals mirror journal contexts and allocations exactly, with unique source links. A private transaction capability permits only the vetted cancellation state changes; callers cannot authorize a bypass with session settings.

No operational database or receipt backup is included in the GitHub source checkpoint. Use the later T16/T17 encrypted backup and verified restore workflows for operational recovery.
